About me


Hello and welcome to my portfolio! I am a web designer, developer, and IT professional who creates websites that are simple, modern, and easy to use. I focus on making websites that work well on all devices, are SEO-friendly, and help people and businesses reach their goals. Over the years, I have worked on many projects such as e-commerce sites, company websites, blogs, and personal portfolios. I enjoy turning ideas into real, working websites using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and other tools.


Apart from building websites, I also help with graphic design, digital marketing, and SEO to improve online presence. I like mixing my technical skills with creativity to give clients the best solutions. Outside work, I love music and DJing, which inspire my designs and creativity. I always keep learning new technology so I can bring fresh ideas and improvements to my work.
